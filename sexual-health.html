What we treat

Three conditions. One quiet conversation.

Each starts with the same short consultation — your doctor decides what is appropriate for you, if anything.

Erectile dysfunction

Proven daily or on-demand options, prescribed after a doctor reviews your history and current medications.

Affects roughly half of men over 40 at some point — you are in ordinary company.

Premature ejaculation

Behavioral techniques plus medical options that actually change timing — not folklore, not distraction tricks.

One of the most common concerns men report, and one of the most responsive to treatment.

Low testosterone

Evaluated properly — your symptoms plus lab work, never guesswork. Treatment only when both point the same way.

Many "low T" symptoms have other causes; a careful workup protects you from the wrong treatment.

Why we ask about everything

Sometimes it’s not about sex at all.

Erectile dysfunction can be an early signal of cardiovascular or metabolic issues — the blood vessels involved are small, so they often show trouble first. That is why our consultation screens your whole history: blood pressure, sugar, medications, sleep — not just the symptom.

Your answers may matter more than you think. If we see something that deserves a closer look, we say so plainly.

How private is private

Discreet is a design choice, not a promise.

  • No phone calls unless you ask

    Everything happens in writing, inside your secure account. If you would rather talk, you choose — we never call unannounced.

  • A billing descriptor that says nothing

    Your card statement shows a neutral descriptor — not a condition, not a medication name.

Questions men actually ask

Asked quietly, answered plainly.

Is this legitimate medicine?

Yes. Your consultation is reviewed by a licensed physician who can decline to prescribe if treatment is not right for you — and sometimes does. The medications we use are the same evidence-based options prescribed in clinics, with the same screening.

Will anyone know?

Not from us. Your chart is encrypted and restricted to your care team, packaging is plain and unmarked, and the billing descriptor is neutral. We contact you only through your secure account unless you ask otherwise.

Is medication safe with my heart condition?

That is exactly what the doctor screens for. Some ED medications interact seriously with nitrates and certain blood pressure treatments, so we ask about your heart, your medications, and your history before anything is prescribed. If it is not safe, we will tell you — and help you understand what to do instead.

What if treatment doesn’t work?

You tell your doctor through your account and the plan changes — a different dose, a different medication, or a closer look at what else might be going on. First attempts do not always land; that is normal, and adjusting is part of the care, not an extra charge for asking.

General information, not personal medical advice. Your physician’s assessment is what determines whether any treatment is appropriate for you.
