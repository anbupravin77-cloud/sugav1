Common questions

Asked before you ask.

Is a real doctor involved?

Yes. Every consultation is reviewed by a licensed physician — a person, not an algorithm. If anything in your chart needs clarifying, the doctor follows up with you directly before deciding on a plan.

Do I need to visit a clinic?

No. The consultation, doctor review, and follow-ups all happen online. If your situation ever needs an in-person exam or lab work, we will tell you honestly and point you to the right place.

Is my information private?

Yes. Your records are encrypted, seen only by your care team, and never sold or shared for marketing. Any deliveries arrive in plain, unmarked packaging.

Will I definitely get medication?

No — and that is deliberate. A consultation is a medical review, not a checkout. If treatment is not clinically appropriate for you, the doctor will say so and explain what makes sense instead.

What does it cost?

The consultation is free. If the doctor recommends treatment, you see clear pricing for your specific plan before you pay anything — no subscriptions you did not agree to, no surprise charges.
